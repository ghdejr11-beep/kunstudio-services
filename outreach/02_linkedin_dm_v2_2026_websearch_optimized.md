# Outreach Script #2 v2 — LinkedIn DM 2026 WebSearch Optimized

**Source**: WebSearch 2026 (5/26) — LinkedIn cold outreach research findings
**Key facts driving v2**:
- Direct cold DM = 99% rejection (avoid pure-cold without warm signal)
- Founder DM = 2-3× reply rate vs sales account (Hong's name OK)
- Prospect content quote = 300% reply rate ↑ (must reference their specific post)
- Connection request ≤ 300 chars (LinkedIn's hard limit)
- First message ≤ 75 words value-first (longer = less reply)
- 2-3 message follow-up over 7-10 days (current scheme = OK)
- Prospect seeing your posts before DM = 60% ↑ connection acceptance (requires daily LinkedIn post by you)
- Multi-channel sync (LinkedIn + email) = 40% ↑ engagement

## ⭐ Variant A v2 — Warm 1st-degree (engaged with launch post)

**Connection request** (≤ 300 chars):
```
Hey [First] — saw you engaged with my Cheonmyeongdang launch on PH. Building small Claude bots for solo founders ($500-1.5k setup). Would love your honest 2-min read on whether the fit's right. — Hong
```

**First DM after connection accept** (≤ 75 words):
```
[First] — quick context (and ask):

I'm shipping 5 Claude bot templates ($500-1.5k setup + $99-299/mo, live in 1-5 days). Email triage, LinkedIn drafts, CRM follow-up, site Q&A, cold email.

Two asks:
1. Anything in your week you'd delete if a bot handled it?
2. Know 1 other founder who'd care? Send them my way.

No pitch deck. Just want honest read.

— Hong
```
