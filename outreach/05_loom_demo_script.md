# Outreach Script #5 — The 90-Second Loom Demo

**Purpose:** Every outreach above ends with "want a Loom?" — this is what you record. Treat the Loom as the demo. Many prospects will buy without a call.

**Why it works:**
- Zero scheduling friction. They watch when they want.
- You record ONE base Loom per bot template, then customize the first 15 sec for each prospect.
- A great Loom does the demo + objection handling + close, in 90 seconds.

---

## Structure (90 seconds, hard cap)

| Time | Section | Words ≈ |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:15 | Personalized hook | 30 |
| 0:15-0:50 | Live demo on their data (or close approximation) | 100 |
| 0:50-1:15 | Price, timeline, scope | 50 |
| 1:15-1:30 | Single clear ask | 30 |

---

## Script template — Email Triage Bot

> **0:00-0:15 — Personalized**
> "Hey [First] — Hong from KunStudio. You'd mentioned [specific thing they said in DM]. So here's what an inbox triage bot would do on a snapshot of your last 20 unread, [date]."

> **0:15-0:50 — Demo (screen-share)**
> *(Show: dashboard with their last 20 emails classified P0/P1/P2/P3. Click into a P1 — show the auto-drafted reply matching their voice. Click into a P3 — show the auto-archive rule.)*
>
> "So this email from [vendor] gets P3, archived without you seeing it. This one from [prospect] gets P1, and I drafted a reply matching your voice — based on 30 of your past sent emails. You hit send or you edit. Daily digest at 9am, like this." *(show digest screenshot)*

> **0:50-1:15 — Price**
> "Setup is $500, monthly is $99 — that's 1 inbox, classification, drafts, and the daily digest. Live in 2 business days. You own the source code, runs on your Hetzner box or mine. Cancel any month."

> **1:15-1:30 — Ask**
> "If this would save you the 30 min/day, reply to the email with 'go' and I'll send a deposit link. If it'd save you 5 min and isn't worth it, tell me — I'd rather hear no fast than hear nothing. Either way, thanks for watching."

---

## Recording rules

1. **One take, no edits.** A polished Loom feels like a pitch. A rough one feels real. Just don't fumble names.
2. **Camera bubble ON.** People buy from faces, not screens.
3. **Use their data (anonymized) or extremely close approximations.** Generic demos lose 50%+ of the magic.
4. **No script in front of you.** Bullet points OK. Reading the script = obvious = trust killer.
5. **One CTA only.** Don't offer "Cal call OR Stripe OR email me". Pick the closest one to closing. If they engaged in DM, the CTA is "reply 'go'". If they were cold, it's the Cal link.
6. **Test the Loom yourself before sending.** Watch the whole 90s. If you'd skip it, re-record.

## Base Looms to record once (then re-use)

| Loom | Use case | Demo data |
|---|---|---|
| **Email Triage** | Anyone with a Gmail | Dummy inbox you set up (20 fake emails) |
| **LinkedIn Posts** | Founders, marketers | Your own LinkedIn — last 30 posts as voice samples |
| **CRM Follow-up** | Sales-led companies | Dummy HubSpot account with 10 fake deals |
| **Sales Q&A** | Anyone with a SaaS site | sajuapp.app — show widget answering 5 Qs |
| **Cold Email** | Anyone doing outbound | 5 real public profiles, 5 generated emails |

**Time to record all 5 base Looms:** ~3 hours. Re-use forever.

---

## Per-prospect customization (~5 min)

For each prospect, re-record JUST the first 15 seconds:
- "Hey [First] — saw [specific thing], let me show you what [bot] would do on [their data/site/list]."

Then splice it onto the base Loom in Loom's editor.

Result: feels 100% custom, costs 5 min/prospect.

---

## What to do with replies

| Reply | Action |
|---|---|
| "Go" / "Send me the deposit link" | Send Stripe Payment Link ($250), reply with onboarding doc, kick off |
| "Have questions" | Cal.com 20-min slot |
| "Not now / wrong timing" | "Got it — mind if I ping you in [N] weeks?" Always say a specific N |
| Ghost after 5 days | One follow-up: "How was the Loom — anything that didn't land?" Then archive |
