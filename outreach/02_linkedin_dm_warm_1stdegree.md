# Outreach Script #2 — LinkedIn DM to 1st-Degree (Warm)

**Target:** Your existing 30+ 1st-degree LinkedIn connections (especially those who liked/commented on your 5/26 launch post).

**Why it works:** Warm DMs convert at 5-10× cold DMs. You're not selling — you're sharing a relevant launch with someone who already chose to be in your network.

---

## Variant A — engaged-with-launch-post

Send to: anyone who liked or commented on the KunStudio launch post (5/26).

```
Hey [First] —

Thanks for the [like / comment about X] on the launch post yesterday — that meant
a lot. I wanted to ping you directly because I'd love your honest take.

I'm building small Claude/GPT bots for solo founders & SMB teams. 5 templates
ready to ship (email triage, LinkedIn posts, CRM follow-up, site Q&A, cold email),
all $500-$1,500 setup + $99-299/mo. Live in 1-5 days.

Two asks:
1. Anything in your week right now you'd happily delete if a bot could handle it?
2. If you know one other founder who'd care about this, mind sending them my way?

No pressure either way — but figured you of all people would tell me straight if
the offer feels off.

— Hong
```

**Why:**
- L1 references their specific action (likes the launch post). Personalization without being weird.
- L2 = the pitch in plain English.
- L3 has TWO low-friction asks (feedback + referral) — they pick one or do both.
- L4 explicitly asks for honest feedback — flips them from "should I reply?" to "I should help".

## Variant B — past-collaborator / commenter

Send to: 1st-degree who'd commented thoughtfully on any post in the last 60 days.

```
Hey [First] —

I'm launching a new thing (small Claude bots for B2B teams, $500 setup + ~$150/mo,
live in days) and you were one of the first people I thought of — your comment on
[specific past post] is part of how I scoped this.

If [specific bot — pick one of 5 based on what you know about their role] would be
useful for [their company], happy to send a 90-sec Loom of how it'd run on your
actual setup. No call required, no pitch deck.

Want one?

— Hong
```

## Variant C — silent-1st-degree (you've never DM'd them)

```
Hey [First] —

I shipped a new thing this week — KunStudio, a 1-person studio that builds small
Claude bots for B2B teams. 5 templates, $500-1,500 setup, live in 1-5 days.

You came to mind because [SPECIFIC reason — their last post / their company /
your shared interest]. If you know anyone wrestling with [the bot's core use case],
I'd appreciate a connection.

And if YOU want a Loom on a use case I think fits [their company], say the word.

— Hong
```

---

## Rules

- Personalize line 1 to THIS person, every time. No template-loop. If you can't find a specific reason, skip them.
- Max **8 DMs/day** (LinkedIn flags faster).
- Never send 2 DMs without a reply. One follow-up max (after 5 days): "Did this fall off your radar?"
- Track replies in a Google Sheet: `name | role | company | sent | replied | loom | call | closed`
- Goal: 2 closes / 30 DMs in week 1.

---

## Subject lines (LinkedIn doesn't show subjects; use these as first-line hooks)

- "thanks for the launch-post like — quick ask"
- "would love your honest read on this"
- "you came to mind for something I shipped"
- "small Claude bot — would it work for [their company]?"
