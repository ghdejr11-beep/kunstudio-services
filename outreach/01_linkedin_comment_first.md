# Outreach Script #1 — LinkedIn "Comment First, DM Later"

**Why it works:** Cold DMs from non-1st-degree connections get ~3% reply. A thoughtful comment that gets a reply from the founder makes you familiar — then the DM lands at ~25-40% reply.

**Target:** B2B founders (1-50 employees) who posted in the last 7 days about a workflow problem (sales, ops, content, customer support).

**Search query on LinkedIn:**
- `("buried in email" OR "drowning in DMs" OR "follow up cold" OR "wrote 5 LinkedIn posts" OR "cold email reply rate") date:past-week`

---

## Step 1 — The comment (post immediately)

Pick ONE of these patterns, never copy-paste both:

### Pattern A (founder-story)
> "Same here. I built a small Claude bot for [specific thing in their post] last quarter and it cut [specific outcome] by [number]. Happy to share the prompt if it'd save you time."

### Pattern B (counter-take)
> "Took a different angle on this — we found that [counter-intuitive finding from your own experience]. Curious if you'd seen the same pattern."

### Pattern C (specific question)
> "What's the part of this that surprised you most? I run something similar and we got blindsided by [specific edge case]."

**Rules:**
- One sentence about THEIR post. One sentence about YOUR experience. End with an invite or a question.
- No "Great post!". No "100%". No "This!". No emojis as the whole comment.
- Don't pitch in the comment. Ever.

---

## Step 2 — Wait for their reply (24-72h)

If they reply → go to Step 3.
If they don't reply but the post is doing well (50+ likes) → comment one more time on a different angle (max 2 comments/person/week).
If they ghost the comment → mark "cold", try a different post in 30 days.

---

## Step 3 — The DM (only after a reply)

Send within 48 hours of their reply. Keep it 3 sentences max.

> Hey [First] —
>
> Thanks for the back-and-forth on the [topic] post. I run a 1-person studio building small Claude bots for B2B teams ($500 setup + ~$150/mo), and [specific bot from KunStudio's 5] would solve the exact loop you described — usually live in 2 days.
>
> Not pitching — happy to send you a 90-sec Loom of how it'd handle your specific case. Want me to record one?

**Why the Loom offer:** lowers the ask from "book a call" (high friction) to "watch a 90-sec video" (zero friction). The Loom itself is the demo.

---

## Step 4 — After Loom sent

If they reply positively → "Want to do a 20-min call to scope this properly? Cal.com link."
If they don't reply → wait 7 days, send ONE more message: "How was the Loom — any questions before I close out my Q3 slots?"
If still no reply → mark cold for 60 days.

---

## Volume guide

- **Mon-Fri**: 5 comments/day on relevant fresh posts = 25/week.
- **Reply rate target**: 30% of comments get a reply from the OP.
- **DM conversion**: 20-30% of replied-to commenters take the Loom.
- **Close**: 10-20% of Loom viewers book a call. 30-50% of calls close.
- **Math**: 25 comments × 30% × 25% × 15% × 40% = ~0.3 closes/week = ~1.2/month = $600-$1,800 in setup + $99-299 in recurring per close.
