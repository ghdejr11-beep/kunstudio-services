# Outreach Script #4 — X / Twitter Reply-Then-DM (Indie Hackers)

**Target:** Solo & 2-person SaaS founders posting on X about wins, struggles, MRR milestones, or stack questions.

**Why X:** Indie hackers reply faster than corporate prospects, $500-1,500 is impulse-buy range, and they share what works publicly (free marketing for you when they tweet about your bot).

**Search queries (`X advanced search`):**
- `(MRR OR ARR) ("0 to" OR "1k" OR "5k") (-rt -filter:replies) min_faves:5`
- `"cold email" (reply OR "open rate") (filter:replies OR "I'm" OR "anyone")`
- `"buried in" (DM OR email OR inbox) -giveaway`
- `("paying customers" OR "first customer") min_faves:10 lang:en`

---

## Step 1 — The reply (in public)

Reply ONCE to their tweet. Keep it ≤200 chars. Bring value, not pitch.

### Pattern A (one specific insight)
> "Same pattern when I went from 0→3K MRR. The unlock was [specific tactic in 8 words]. Took 3 weeks to dial in, 10 min/day after that."

### Pattern B (a tool/prompt they could use today)
> "If it helps — I run a Claude prompt that does [exact thing they're struggling with]. Happy to paste it as a reply, just say the word."

### Pattern C (one counter-question)
> "Curious — what's your reply-rate on [the segment they're sending to]? I see a huge gap when I split [criterion A] vs [criterion B]."

---

## Step 2 — Watch for reply or like

- Reply to your reply → go to Step 3.
- Like only → wait 24h, send a soft DM (Step 3 with adjusted opener).
- Nothing → no DM. Move on.

---

## Step 3 — The DM (only after they engage)

```
Hey — appreciated the back-and-forth on the [topic] thread.

Since you mentioned [SPECIFIC THING], wanted to share: I'm a 1-person studio
building small Claude bots ($500 setup, $99-299/mo) and [SPECIFIC bot from
the 5] would solve that exact problem. Live in 2-4 days, you own the code.

Want a 90-sec Loom of how it'd run on your stack? Not a pitch — just the
shortest way to see if it's a fit.

— Hong, KunStudio (sajuapp.app, korlens.app)
```

**DO NOT:**
- Pitch in the public reply (kills both the reply and the DM).
- Send if you have nothing specific from their thread (template-spray = ban risk).
- Send a calendar link in the first DM. Loom > Cal link.

---

## Sample real reply chains (templates you can adapt)

### Their tweet: "Sent 200 cold emails. 3 replies. WTF am I doing wrong."

**Your public reply:**
> "Subject lines are 60% of it. Mine 2x'd when I switched from 'curious about X' (overused) to a literal question from their last LinkedIn post. Want me to DM you 5 of my best subjects?"

→ They reply "yes please" → DM:

> "Here they are — copy & paste, just swap the brackets:
> 1. about your post on [topic]
> 2. one question on your [feature/launch]
> 3. [their name] — quick read?
> 4. [specific number from their company] question
> 5. saw your [PH/blog/LI thing]
>
> Also — these came out of a Claude bot I built that personalizes each cold email per lead (LinkedIn post + company news + real hook). $500 setup, $129/mo for 500/mo. Want a Loom?"

### Their tweet: "AT 50 PAYING USERS 🎉 grind doesn't stop"

**Your public reply:**
> "Huge. 50→500 the hardest part is everything you handled by hand starts breaking. What's the one thing you're already dreading scaling?"

→ They reply "honestly support email" → DM:

> "Yep — that one hit me at 80. I built a small Claude bot that triages Gmail (P0/P1/P2) and drafts replies in my voice. $500 setup + $99/mo, live in 2 days, runs forever after.
> Want me to run it on a snapshot of your last 20 support emails and send back a Loom?"

---

## Volume guide

- **5-10 thoughtful replies/day** beats 50 sprayed ones every time.
- X's algorithm rewards quality engagement — your reply gets seen by their whole audience.
- Target: 1 DM conversation/day = 5-7 quality DMs/week = 1-2 closes/month from X alone.
